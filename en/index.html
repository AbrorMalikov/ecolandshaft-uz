FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Answers to frequently asked questions

How much does 1 m² of artificial turf and natural roll sod cost? +

Fresh natural roll turf from our nursery ranges between 9,000 and 30,000 UZS/m² depending on volume. Artificial grass with pile height from 10 to 50 mm ranges from 48,800 to 148,000 UZS/m².

What is the turnaround time for a turnkey mini-football pitch? +

A standard 20×40m (800 m²) mini-football pitch complete with drainage foundation, steel fencing, netting, LED floodlights, and FIFA 50mm turf is constructed in 15 to 25 days.

What warranty is provided for playground rubber flooring? +

All seamless SBR and virgin EPDM rubber surfaces are backed by an official 12-month contractual warranty.

Do you deliver materials and perform installation across all Uzbekistan provinces? +

Yes, we deliver from our Tashkent warehouse to any province within 24 hours, and our specialized construction crews carry out turnkey installation on-site.
