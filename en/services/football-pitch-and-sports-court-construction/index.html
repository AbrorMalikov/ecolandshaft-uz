WORK PROCESS

Project execution in 4 systematic steps

PHASE 01

Excavation & Base Grading

Grading 25–35 cm depth, slope drainage channels, and geotextile membrane placement.

PHASE 02

Stone & Asphalt Drainage Layer

Dual-fraction crushed stone compaction, concrete curbing, and perimeter anchoring.

PHASE 03

Fence & LED Tower Assembly

Welding 60×60mm steel poles, protective primer coating, and commercial netting.

PHASE 04

FIFA Turf Laying & Infill

Specialist thermal seam gluing, white field marking, and quartz sand + rubber granule infill.
